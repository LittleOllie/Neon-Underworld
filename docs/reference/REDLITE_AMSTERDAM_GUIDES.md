## 1. Overview

In this edition of Redlite, you can make money from your whores working on the streets, or working in brothels which you can purchase from the market. You can also purchase shops, in which you are able to sell your drugs at a higher rate than in the sell items menu.

You will need to make sure your whores are happy by maintaining a supply of **thugs**, **condoms**, and **hash**. Without these three items — or if you run low on any of them — your prostitutes can walk out on you when you are using turns to either make drugs or scout hoes.

As you get more whores from scouting, off the market, or by stealing them from other players, you will also need to keep a larger supply of condoms, thugs, and hash in order to keep your prostitutes happy.

You can also change your prostitutes' payout level in order to make them happier, and to make yourself more money from your whores.

## 2. Turns

When you start the game you will be given a few turns. After that you will automatically receive **2 turns every 6 minutes**. It is also possible to buy turns using PayPal through the buy items menu in the game.

Your turns can be used in two ways:

### Make drugs

The amount of drugs you will make depends on:

- The amount of turns you use
- The amount of thugs you have (thugs make the drugs for you)

Your whores will also make you cash when you are making drugs.

### Scout

When using turns to scout, there are **five different areas** that you can send your thugs to scout in each city. You will recruit both thugs and whores to add to your criminal empire. The amount of each will vary depending on where you choose to scout and the amount of turns you use.

Your whores will make you cash when scouting turns.

**Remember to use your turns wisely.**

## 3. Prostitute / Thug Happiness

It is essential that you make sure your prostitutes have a high level of happiness; otherwise your whores will walk out on you, leaving you no way of making money.

Keeping your prostitutes happy revolves around having a solid supply of **drugs**, **condoms**, and **thugs**, and also the **payout rate** which you set for them.

It is best if you can get your payout at **1%** so you are making the maximum amount of money from your whores, and their happiness to be **100%** so you don't have whores walking out on you.

Prostitute happiness influences:

- How successful you will be when scouting for more whores
- Whether your whores will walk out on you when you are using turns

### Thug happiness

In order to keep your thugs happy, you will need to keep at least **1 gun per thug**. Guns range from glocks to AK-47's, with AK-47's being the best. You will also need to have a supply of **beer**; otherwise, like your whores, your thugs will walk out on you too.

### Starting supplies

When you initially start the game, you will start with a small sum of cash. You will need to buy some guns, beer, condoms, and hash to make sure that your thugs and whores are happy, and then you can start to use your turns.

If you keep a lot of condoms (which doesn't add to your net worth), you will need to keep less hash to make your whores happy. Hash adds a lot to your net worth and would make you vulnerable to other players if you were to have too much hash.

## 4. Distrikt Market

The Distrikt Market can be used to sell and buy items to and from other players. Bigger players can also payout large sums of cash for items they want to buy — you can payout **billions of dollars** through the market to other players.

The system will also randomly place items on the market for players to purchase. You can purchase many items on the market, and most are for a lower price than they are from the buy items menu.

### Items you can buy from the market

- Whores
- Thugs
- Rides
- AK's
- Hash
- Shrooms
- Coke
- Heroin

Brothels and coffee shops are also placed on the market randomly by the system. **You cannot sell brothels or coffee shops on the market.**

### Bidding mechanics

Items on the market can get very overpriced, as each time somebody bids on an item, the price rises by **20%**. Be careful that you don't pay too much for an item.

If you are selling an item to another player, you can ask for a certain price, and then with the help from another player they will bid up the item so it raises in price 20% every bid until it reaches the price you agreed to sell for.

### Starting prices

| Item | Starting price |
|------|----------------|
| 1 whore | $1,750 |
| 1 thug | $700 |
| 1 ride | $2,000 |
| 1 AK | $3,240 |
| 1 hash | $1 |
| 1 shroom | $5 |
| 1 coke | $9 |
| 1 heroin | $13 |
| Brothels and shops | $5,000 |

Using the market to purchase items will help you grow bigger a lot faster, as you can purchase these items at a lower price than anywhere else.

### Risk

You can become vulnerable when buying items on the market. If people see that you are paying a large amount of cash for an item, they can outbid you and then invade your home to take your cash — so watch your back when buying items.

The same applies if you are selling: if other players see you selling an item for a lot of cash, they can wait until the item sells, then invade your home to take the cash.

**It is best if you are online when you get big sums of cash, and spend it quickly.**

## 5. Net Worth / Rankings

Your net worth is made up by the amount of **thugs**, **whores**, **vehicles**, **drugs**, and **cash** that you have on hand.

Your **weapons**, **beer**, **condoms**, and **shops / brothels do not add to your net worth**.

### Asset values

| Asset | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Prostitutes | $1,750 each |
| Thugs | $700 each |
| Rides | $2,000 each |
| Hash | $5 each |
| Shrooms | $5 each |
| Coke | $5 each |
| Heroin | $5 each |

Thugs, prostitutes, and vehicles increase your net worth by the largest amount.

However, you will find that you are better off if you keep your net worth as **low as you can** in order to avoid being attacked by bigger players.

All players are ranked in order of net worth — the player with the higher net worth will be higher in the ranks. Obviously the aim of the game is to come 1st place in the ranks, but having a higher net worth than necessary will make you more of a target for other players to attack you.

## 6. Cartels

A cartel is a group of up to **5 players** who work as a team. The idea is to have as many players in your cartel as you can in order for the cartel to grow quickly.

When you are in a cartel, the **creator of the cartel** is the one who is in charge of it.

### Donations

All players in the cartel will donate a percentage of:

- Their drugs made when making drugs
- The cash their whores make when using turns

The percentage you donate is decided by the leader of the cartel, and can range from **0% to 60%**.

### Cartel assets

The drugs and cash donated to a cartel by cartel members can be used to buy thugs, Uzi's, and glocks. The cartel thugs are there to protect you when other players try to drive-by attack you.

In order for a person to win a drive-by attack on you, they will need to have more thugs than **your thugs plus your cartel thugs** combined.

Your cartel thugs get killed in attacks, but the cartel guns you will never lose unless you sell them. Any sensible cartel leader will only buy Uzi's for the cartel thugs; the same as a sensible player will only pack AK's.

The drugs in your cartel can also be donated to players in the cartel in order to help them if they have been attacked, or to help them build thugs and guns to attack other players.

You can only donate drugs **twice every twelve hours**, and only the cartel leader or co-leader can donate drugs.

### Advantages and disadvantages

**Best advantage:** The protection you get from your cartel thugs.

**Disadvantage:** You could be losing out on 0–60% of your drugs and cash. Make sure that if you are in a cartel you are not donating too much of your assets. If you feel the cut is too high, simply leave the cartel.

### Rules

- Cartels have a limit of **5 players**
- Cartel members can only tag-team another player in an attack with their **own cartel members**
- Tag-teaming with a player from another cartel can result in warnings or bans
- The 5-player limit prevents unfair tag-teaming (e.g. 3 cartels vs. 1 cartel)
- Once in a cartel, it is **against the rules to buy or sell items with any cartel members** — violations can result in warnings or eviction

## 7. Attacks

In order to succeed in the game you will need to attack players.

### Reasons to attack

- A player has a high net worth, indicating they are holding cash or drugs
- Revenge for an earlier attack on you or a cartel member
- Steal rides or whores
- Attack brothels or coffee shops to steal drugs or cash
- Kill thugs on a larger player to lower their net worth so you can attack someone lower in the ranks

### Attack range

You can only attack players within a certain net worth range:

> **Half your net worth to double your net worth**

Example: if your net worth was $100 million, you could attack someone with a net worth of $50–$200 million.

### Weapons

The guns you are packing makes a huge difference on the outcome of an attack. You can purchase glocks, Uzi's, and AK's. For the best outcome, pack **100% AK's**; for the worst, **100% glocks**. Uzi's are somewhere in the middle.

| Weapon | Firing capacity |
|--------|-----------------|
| Glocks | 2 bullets |
| Uzi's | 25 bullets |
| AK's | 45 bullets |

### Attack types

#### Drive By Shooting

The most effective way to kill a large amount of a player's thugs. In some cases you can kill all of a player's thugs in one attack — or if you attack someone with more thugs than you, you can end up killing all of your own thugs too.

- Requires **1 vehicle for every 5 thugs** sent on the attack
- Example: 100,000 thugs requires 20,000 vehicles
- Must have more thugs than the target's thugs **plus cartel thugs** combined to win

#### Home Invasion

Generally used to steal cash. Can also be used to kill players with large amounts of cartel thugs (where a drive-by would fail).

Players tend to use home invasions to take cash from others seen receiving large amounts through the market. A great way to gain sums of cash and setback another player.

#### Raid Drugs Labs

Similar to a home invasion — kills some thugs. If you win, you steal **drugs** instead of cash.

Used when a player's net worth is higher than it should be. If a home invasion yields little cash, the excess net worth is likely drugs.

#### Steal Whores

First kill their thugs (drive-by, home invasion, or raid drugs labs). Then send **coke or heroin** to try to steal whores.

The amount stolen depends on how many whores the target has and how much coke/heroin you send. Large amounts can kill some or all of a player's whores — deliberately called a **"crackbomb"** when used to destroy income.

#### Jack Vehicles

Similarly to stealing whores, you must kill all thugs first. For every ride you wish to steal, you need a thug to bring it back.

Example: to steal 10,000 rides, sell 10,000 of your own thugs into 2,500 of your own rides.

#### Brothel Invasions

Used to gain cash held in a brothel, kill thugs, and kill whores in the brothel.

- Requires **1 ride for every 5 thugs** sent
- Choose which brothel to attack and how many thugs to send
- Brothels only generate cash when using turns — attack when you notice another player using turns
- Cash can be stored in brothels even when offline

#### Rob Coffee Shop

Like brothel invasion — **1 ride per 5 thugs**. Used to steal drugs and cash from a coffee shop.

Choose which coffee shop to attack and how many thugs to send.

### Defending yourself

Reasons others will attack you:

- Your net worth is too high
- Revenge
- To steal rides or whores

**Best defence:** Pack **100% AK's**.

Reduce vulnerability by:

- Not maintaining an unnecessarily high net worth
- If holding large amounts of whores, maintain large amounts of thugs too

To protect whores from being stolen: raise prostitute payout to **100%** while not using turns and hold a lot of condoms — whores stay happy even with few thugs. **Remember to lower payout when using turns again.**

## 8. Travel

You can travel around **three different cities** within the game.

| Player type | Travels per round |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Members | 90 |
| Non-members | 20 |

### Cities

- **Walletjes**
- **Singel**
- **Pijp**

Each city has different advantages and disadvantages. You may recruit more whores and thugs in one city, and make more drugs in another — something you will have to work out for yourself.

### Travel costs

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Thugs travelling by train | $200 each |
| Thugs travelling in your rides | Free |
| Whores travelling by train | $30 each |
| Coffee shops you wish to move | $3,000 each |
| Brothels you wish to move | $5,000 each |

Additional requirements:

- Costs **10 turns** to move to another city
- **Not possible to move a cartel** — promote a new leader before travelling
- Must **remove whores from brothels** before you travel
- You will **not make any cash** when using the 10 turns to travel

## 9. Brothels

Brothels can be purchased from the market at a starting price of **$5,000**, raised by 20% every time a player bids. Brothels are randomly placed on the market by the system — keep an eye out for them.

Brothels range from **type 1 to type 5 (T-1 – T-5)**. T-1 makes the least cash; T-5 makes the most.

The way to make the most cash in your brothel is to have as many whores working in it as possible, and charge as much as you can.

### Brothel types

| Type | Whore capacity | Rate range |
|------|----------------|------------|
| T-1 | 0–50 | $0 – $50 |
| T-2 | 0–100 | $0 – $125 |
| T-3 | 0–200 | $0 – $250 |
| T-4 | 0–400 | $0 – $325 |
| T-5 | 0–800 | $0 – $500 |

Your brothels will automatically make you cash when you are using turns. You will **not** automatically receive the cash — you need to **manually collect** cash from each individual brothel. This costs **1 turn per brothel** to collect.

---

## 10. Shops

Coffee shops can be purchased from the market the same as brothels, with a starting market price of **$5,000**. They are also placed on the market randomly by the system.

There are **four different types** of coffee shops, one for each drug type you can make or purchase.

### Shop types and price ranges

| Shop type | Sell price range |
|-----------|------------------|
| Hash coffee shop | $0 – $30 |
| Shroom coffee shop | $0 – $75 |
| Coke coffee shop | $0 – $95 |
| Heroin coffee shop | $0 – $110 |

To make cash from your coffee shops:

1. Deliver drugs to the shops
2. Set a rate at which you wish to sell the drug (highest price possible is best)

The same as brothels, your coffee shops make cash automatically when using turns. You must **collect the cash yourself**, costing **1 turn per coffee shop**.
